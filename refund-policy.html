Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Refund Policy applies to purchases made through SEPLUS, operated by Global Computers & CCTV Solutions.

1. Refund Eligibility

A refund may be considered in the following situations:

  • An eligible order is cancelled before it enters processing or fulfilment.
  • Payment is successfully received but the order cannot be fulfilled.
  • The customer receives a damaged, defective, or incorrect product and the issue is verified.
  • Another refund is required under applicable law or is approved by Global Computers & CCTV Solutions.

2. Cancellation and Refund of Paid Orders

If a paid order is eligible for cancellation and the cancellation is approved, the applicable refund will be initiated to the original payment method used for the transaction.

3. Damaged, Defective or Incorrect Products

If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect product, contact us with your order number and relevant details. We will review the issue and, where applicable, arrange the appropriate resolution, which may include replacement or refund.

4. Non-Refundable Situations

A refund may not be available where a product has been used, altered, physically damaged after delivery, or where the request does not satisfy the applicable product or order conditions.

5. Refund Processing Timeline

Once a refund is approved, Global Computers & CCTV Solutions will initiate the refund within 5–7 business days. The time taken for the amount to appear in the customer's account may additionally depend on the payment gateway, bank, card issuer, or other payment service provider.

6. Payment Gateway and Failed Payments

If a payment fails or an amount is debited but the order is not successfully completed, the transaction will be reviewed. Any eligible reversal or refund will be processed through the applicable payment service provider and original payment method.

7. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, contact us with your order number, registered name, mobile number, and a description of the reason for the request. Requests are reviewed before a refund is approved.
