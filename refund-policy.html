Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Refund Policy applies to purchases made through SEPLUS, operated by Global Computers & CCTV Solutions.

1. Refund Eligibility

A refund may be considered when an order is cancelled before fulfilment, a payment is successfully received but the order cannot be fulfilled, or another refund circumstance is approved by us under applicable law.

2. Order Cancellation and Refund

If an eligible order is cancelled and a refund is approved, the refund will be initiated through the applicable payment method or payment service. Processing time may depend on the payment provider and banking network.

3. Damaged or Incorrect Products

If you receive a damaged, defective or incorrect product, contact us promptly with the order details and supporting information. We will review the request and provide the applicable resolution.

4. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds may not be available where the product has been used, altered, damaged after delivery, or where the request does not meet the applicable return or refund conditions.

5. Refund Processing

Once an approved refund is initiated, the actual credit time is controlled by the payment gateway, bank or card issuer.

6. Contact

For refund requests, please provide your order number and registered contact details when contacting Global Computers & CCTV Solutions.
