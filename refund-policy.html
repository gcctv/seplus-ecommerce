Refund Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

SEPLUS is an online store operated by Global Computers & CCTV Solutions.

1. Refund Policy

We provide refunds for eligible orders in accordance with the conditions stated on this page. Refund eligibility depends on the order status, payment status, product condition and the reason for the refund request.

2. Eligible Refunds

  • An order is paid for but cannot be fulfilled by us.
  • An eligible order is cancelled before processing or fulfilment.
  • The customer receives an incorrect, damaged or defective product and the issue is verified by us.
  • A refund is otherwise required under applicable law.

3. Returns for Incorrect, Damaged or Defective Products

If a customer receives an incorrect, damaged or defective product, the customer should contact us with the order number and details of the issue. We will review the request and, where applicable, provide a replacement or refund.

4. Refund Not Applicable

A refund may not be available for products that have been used, modified or physically damaged after delivery, or where the request does not meet the applicable product or order conditions.

5. Refund Processing Time

For an approved refund, the refund will be initiated within 5–7 business days. After initiation, the time required for the amount to appear in the customer's account may depend on the payment gateway, bank, card issuer or other payment service provider.

6. Refund Method

For online payments, an approved refund will be processed to the original payment method used for the transaction, where supported by the payment service provider.

7. Failed or Unsuccessful Payments

If a payment fails and no successful payment is received by us, no product refund is due. If money is debited from the customer's account despite an unsuccessful payment, the transaction will be handled through the payment gateway and banking process applicable to that transaction.

8. How to Request a Refund

Customers should provide the order number, registered name, mobile number and reason for the refund request. We will review the request and communicate the applicable resolution.
