Refund Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

SEPLUS is an online store operated by Global Computers & CCTV Solutions.

1. Refund Eligibility

We provide refunds for eligible orders based on the order status, payment status, product condition and the reason for the refund request.

  • A successful payment was received but the order cannot be fulfilled.
  • An eligible order was cancelled before processing or fulfilment.
  • The customer received an incorrect, damaged or defective product and the issue is verified by us.
  • A refund is required under applicable law.

2. Incorrect, Damaged or Defective Products

If an incorrect, damaged or defective product is received, contact us with the order number and details of the issue. We will review the request and, where applicable, arrange a replacement or refund.

3. Cases Where a Refund May Not Apply

A refund may not be available where a product has been used, modified or physically damaged after delivery, or where the request does not satisfy the applicable product or order conditions.

4. Refund Processing Time

For an approved refund, we will initiate the refund within 5–7 business days. After initiation, the time for the amount to appear in the customer's account can depend on the payment gateway, bank, card issuer or other payment service provider.

5. Refund Method

For online payments, approved refunds will normally be processed to the original payment method used for the transaction, where supported by the payment service provider.

6. Failed or Unsuccessful Payments

If a payment fails and no successful payment is received by us, no product refund is due. If the customer's account is debited despite an unsuccessful payment, the transaction will be handled through the applicable payment gateway and banking process.

7. How to Request a Refund

Please provide the order number, registered name, mobile number and reason for the request. We will review the request and communicate the applicable resolution.
