Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GLOBALLIX handles information when you create an account, browse products, place an order, or contact us. Please read it before using the website.

1. Information we may collect

  • Account details: name, mobile number, email address, and authentication-related account information.
  • Order and delivery details: products, quantities, order status, delivery address, city, state, and pincode.
  • Payment-related information: payment status and transaction/order references needed to process and support a purchase. Online payment details are handled through the payment provider shown at checkout.
  • Technical and cart information: items kept in your browser's local storage to support the shopping cart, and basic technical information needed for site operation and security.
  • Communications: information you provide when asking for order help or contacting support.

2. How we use information

Information may be used to create and maintain your account, display saved address details, check stock, process orders and payments, arrange delivery, send transactional emails, respond to support requests, prevent fraud or misuse, maintain the website, and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Service providers and sharing

GLOBALLIX uses third-party services to operate website features, including Supabase for authentication/database functions and the payment provider presented at checkout. Information may be shared with service providers, delivery partners, or authorities when reasonably necessary to provide a requested service, protect rights or safety, or comply with law. Service providers may process information under their own terms and privacy notices.

4. Browser storage

The website uses browser local storage to remember cart items between pages. Clearing browser storage may remove your cart. Authentication or other technical storage may also be used by the services required for the site to function.

5. Retention and security

Information is kept for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including order support, recordkeeping, security, and legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online system can be guaranteed completely secure. Do not share your password or one-time verification codes with anyone.

6. Your choices and requests

You can keep your account and delivery details accurate by updating them through available account features or contacting GLOBALLIX through its published support channel. You may also contact us with questions about your personal information. Certain order or transaction records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate recordkeeping purposes.

7. Children

The website is intended for customers able to enter into purchases under applicable law. A parent or guardian should contact GLOBALLIX if they believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately.

8. Policy updates

We may update this policy as website features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The current version and its last-updated date will be shown on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the official support contact details published on the GLOBALLIX website. Before production launch, add and verify a monitored support email or other contact method and confirm this policy reflects the actual data flows and retention practices of your website.
