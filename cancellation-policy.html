Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Cancellation Policy applies to orders placed through SEPLUS, operated by Global Computers & CCTV Solutions.

1. Customer Cancellation

Customers may request cancellation of an order while cancellation is available through the website or before the order has progressed to fulfilment. Cancellation availability may depend on the order status.

2. Paid Orders

If payment has been completed and an eligible cancellation is approved, the applicable refund will be initiated according to our Refund Policy.

3. Orders Already Processed

An order that has already been processed, dispatched or otherwise fulfilled may not be cancellable through the website. In such cases, please contact us for assistance.

4. Payment Failure or Abandonment

If an online payment is unsuccessful or a payment session is abandoned, the order may be cancelled according to the website's payment and order-processing workflow.

5. Contact

For cancellation requests, please provide your order number and registered contact details when contacting Global Computers & CCTV Solutions.
