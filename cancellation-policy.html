Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Cancellation Policy applies to orders placed through SEPLUS, operated by Global Computers & CCTV Solutions.

1. Customer Cancellation

Customers may request cancellation while an order is in New Order status and before the order enters processing or fulfilment. Cancellation availability is based on the current order status and fulfilment stage.

2. Orders in Processing or Fulfilment

Once an order has moved to Processing, been dispatched, or otherwise entered fulfilment, cancellation through the website may no longer be available. Customers should contact us for assistance in such cases.

3. Paid Orders

If payment has been completed and an eligible cancellation is approved, the applicable refund will be initiated to the original payment method in accordance with our Refund Policy.

4. Unpaid or Failed Payment Orders

If an online payment fails, is cancelled by the customer, or the payment session is abandoned, the related unpaid order may be cancelled according to the website's order-processing workflow. No refund is required where no payment was successfully received.

5. Cancellation by SEPLUS

SEPLUS may cancel an order when the product cannot be fulfilled, required information is materially incorrect, or a payment/order issue prevents fulfilment. If payment was successfully received for an order cancelled by us and a refund is applicable, the refund will be initiated to the original payment method.

6. Refund Timeline After Cancellation

For an approved cancellation of a paid order, the refund will be initiated within 5–7 business days. The final credit time may depend on the payment gateway, bank, card issuer, or other payment service provider.

7. How to Request Cancellation

Where the website provides a cancellation option, customers can use it from their order page while the order is eligible. For other cancellation requests, contact us with the order number and registered contact details.
