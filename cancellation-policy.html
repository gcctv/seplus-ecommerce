Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

SEPLUS is an online store operated by Global Computers & CCTV Solutions.

1. Customer Order Cancellation

Customers may cancel an eligible order from the order page while the order is in New Order status and before it moves to processing or fulfilment.

2. Orders Already in Processing

Once an order has changed to Processing, been dispatched or entered fulfilment, the online cancellation option may no longer be available. Customers may contact us for assistance.

3. Paid Orders Cancelled by the Customer

If a paid order is eligible for cancellation and the cancellation is approved, the applicable refund will be initiated to the original payment method in accordance with our Refund Policy.

4. Unpaid or Failed Payments

If an order has not been successfully paid for, or a payment attempt fails or is abandoned, the unpaid order may be cancelled. No product refund is required where no successful payment was received.

5. Cancellation by SEPLUS

We may cancel an order when the product cannot be fulfilled or an order/payment issue prevents fulfilment. If a successful payment was received and a refund is applicable, the refund will be initiated to the original payment method.

6. Refund After Cancellation

For an approved cancellation of a paid order, the refund will be initiated within 5–7 business days. The final credit time may depend on the payment gateway, bank, card issuer or other payment service provider.

7. How to Request Cancellation

Where the cancellation option is available, customers can cancel an eligible order from their order page. For other requests, contact us with the order number and registered contact details.
